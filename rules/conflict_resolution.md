# Conflict Resolution Rules

Based on strict document ingestion, the following priority logic must be applied during analysis:

## 1. Core Number Overrides
- **Karmic Debts (4, 8, 13, 16, 26, 28)**: These numbers always trigger mandatory warnings and override optimistic traits in compound or compatibility analysis.
- **Sun Worship Exclusion**: If Birth Number is 8 AND Destiny Number is 9, sun-related remedies (like Argh) are strictly prohibited.
- **Name Correction Priority**: If a name total is 4 or 8 AND the person's birth number is 4 or 8, the name MUST be changed. This overrides any positive letter vibrations within that name.

## 2. Temporal Overrides
- **Personal Year vs. Compound Numbers**: Personal Year traits override long-term compound number traits for current timing. For example, a lucky compound number 60 cannot override the restrictions of Personal Year 4 or 9.
- **Lost Opportunities**: Opportunities in Personal Year 1 are prioritized. If lost, they are considered blocked for the next 9-year cycle regardless of other lucky numbers.

## 3. Compatibility Overrides
- **Enemy Numbers**: If a partner's psychic number is an "Enemy" to the subject's psychic number, this overrides general zodiac compatibility (Green/OK).
- **Opposite Numbers**: Psychic-Destiny internal conflicts (e.g., 1-8) override any optimistic 81-combination ratings.

## 4. Business & Branding
- **Proprietor Grid Priority**: Business name harmony is calculated against the Proprietor's Birth and Destiny numbers first. Business partner grids are secondary.
- **Mobile Digit Restriction**: The presence of digits 4 or 8 in a mobile number triggers a "Malefic" warning regardless of the total sum being a lucky number.

## 5. Calculation Integrity
- **Master Numbers**: 11, 22, and 33 must never be reduced during intermediate calculation steps. Their presence overrides standard single-digit reduction logic.
- **Single Digit Stop**: In name numerology, if the first row of a name sum results in a single digit, further reduction is blocked.
